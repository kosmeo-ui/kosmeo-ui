A list whose `InfiniteScroll` menu has a default sort or pre-selected filters now makes one request on mount instead of two or three. `useInfinite`, `useInfiniteCursor` and `useInfiniteArchivableCursor` wait for the first `onQueryChange` before fetching, and fetch their default page (`limit=…`) on their own when nothing sends one; they report `isLoading` until then, so the list shows its loading state rather than "No results found". `InfiniteScroll` sends its menu's sort and filters in its first query.
